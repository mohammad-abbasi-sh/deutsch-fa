# Gender-prediction rules (Genusregeln)

Persian has no grammatical gender, so these rules are the learner's
compass — but they are **heuristics with stated reliability**, not laws.
The tool's `rules` command applies them in this order; when a noun is in
the curated list, the dictionary always wins and the tool says so.

## Very reliable (خیلی مطمئن)

- **-ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -tät, -tion, -sion, -ik → feminine.**
  *die Wohnung, die Freiheit, die Möglichkeit, die Freundschaft,
  die Universität, die Diskussion, die Musik.*
- **-chen, -lein → neuter.** Diminutives are always neuter —
  *das Mädchen, das Brötchen, das Fräulein.* ⚠ *-chen in **Kuchen** is not
  a diminutive — der Kuchen is masculine.*

## Reliable (مطمئن)

- **-ismus, -ling, -or → masculine.**
  *der Tourismus, der Lehrling, der Motor.*
- **Days, months, seasons → masculine.**
  *der Montag, der Januar, der Sommer.* (Compass points and most weather
  words too: *der Norden, der Regen* — tendency, not law.)

## Strong tendency (گرایش قوی)

- **-e → feminine.** *die Lampe, die Tasche, die Woche.*
  Memorize the exceptions: **der Junge, der Name, der Käse** (and the
  neuter *das Ende, das Auge*).

## Tendency (گرایش)

- **-ei → feminine** (*die Bäckerei* — exception: *das Ei*).
- **-anz / -enz → feminine** (*die Distanz, die Konferenz*).
- **-ur → feminine** (*die Natur, die Kultur*).
- **-age / -ade → feminine** (*die Garage, die Limonade*).
- **-ment → neuter** (*das Dokument, das Instrument*).
- **Ge-…-e → neuter** (*das Gebäude, das Gespräch*).
- **-o → neuter** (*das Auto, das Kino* — exception: *die Disco*).

## Mostly (غالباً — with famous exceptions)

- **-nis → neuter** (*das Ergebnis, das Zeugnis* — but **die Kenntnis,
  die Erlaubnis** are feminine).
- **-tum → neuter** (*das Eigentum* — but **der Reichtum, der Irrtum**
  are masculine).

## No rule helps — memorize

*der Mann, die Frau, das Kind, das Haus, der Tisch, das Wasser …*
The honest rule from TalkPal's German teachers: **always learn the noun
with its article** — not *Haus*, but *das Haus*.
